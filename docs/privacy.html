SemaTor privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy covers the SemaTor app for Android (including the Google Play version, package org.semator.app) and the SemaTor desktop versions for Windows and Linux.

SemaTor does not collect personal data. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting service, and the developer runs no servers that the app reports to.

What stays on your device

  • Your library, disk images, saved games, settings, touch-control layouts, music and cover pictures are stored on your device, in SemaTor's own folder. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Games you add with Add games are copied from the files or folder you pick into SemaTor's folder on your device. SemaTor only sees the files you choose.
  • Diagnostics: SemaTor writes a diagnostics file only when you choose Save diagnostics. The file is saved on your device and is never sent automatically. If you choose to share it (for example to report a problem), you pick the app and the recipient, and it is then handled by them. Diagnostics describe the app and the device (app version, settings, device model and performance figures); storage paths are shortened.

Internet use

SemaTor uses the internet only for actions you start:

  • Download artwork fetches public cover pictures from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). The request names the picture being fetched and contains nothing else about you.
  • Links, such as to music websites or this site, open in your web browser under that website's own policy.
  • On the desktop versions, checking for updates contacts GitHub to read the latest release. The Google Play version is updated by Google Play.

Like any web request, these connections reveal your IP address to the server that answers them. SemaTor sends them no other information.

Permissions (Android)

  • Internet: only for the actions above.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: to keep the Jukebox playing in the background.
  • Vibration: touch-control feedback.
  • All files access is requested only by versions distributed outside Google Play, to read your disk images and music where you keep them. The Google Play version does not request it.

Children

SemaTor is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub or ask on Discord.
