SemaTor

An Atari ST translation layer. Not an emulator.

The 68000 code executes, the ST's hardware and operating-system interface are reproduced, and where a game's hot routines are understood they can be replaced with native code that does the same job faster. No TOS ROM needed — none included, none required.

What makes it different

Emulators aim to run everything by being a faithful machine. SemaTor aims to run games well — and for the ones it knows, better than the original hardware could.

Native routines, audited

A profile can mark hot 68000 routines for native replacement. --audit-native runs both, keeps the 68000 answer, and reports every disagreement — a replacement cannot change behaviour unnoticed.

Enhancements, per game

Cleaner scrolling, extended play areas, audio fixes, cheats. Each a switch, and --safe turns them all off.

No TOS

GEMDOS, BIOS and XBIOS are implemented directly. That is why no ROM is needed — and why it is not a copy of one.

Frame generation

Optional generated in-between frames on the GPU, with a native optical-flow path on NVIDIA and a compute path elsewhere.

A picture worth the pixels

Sharp pixels, simple filters, or a full CRT studio — beam, phosphor, mask, glass, colour — with HDR where the display offers it.

Built for two screens

On a dual-screen handheld the game runs on top while the menus, the controls and a per-game page live on the lower panel.

The library

One place for your disks: lists, covers, saved games with previews, search as you type, and a layout that suits the screen it is on.

Before you download

  • No games are included, and none ever will be. Bring your own disk images.
  • Compatibility is per game. Eight titles have profiles and have been worked on in depth; everything else runs on the general machine and may be perfect, imperfect, or not start.
  • Frame generation is experimental on mobile. Original frames are recommended there and are one press away.
  • Filters depend on your GPU driver. The chain checks its own output and steps aside rather than leaving a black screen.
  • Android needs “All files access” to read your games folder.

SemaTor contains no Atari TOS ROM and requires none, and no game code, graphics, audio or data of any kind. What it is and is not is set out in COPYRIGHT.txt, written by the people who built it — not legal advice.
