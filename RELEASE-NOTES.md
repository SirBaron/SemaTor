# 1.1.616 — Powermonger camera controls, Star Wars and Empire flight

### Powermonger

- **Mouse and camera.** The wheel zooms continuously between the original camera levels. Houses follow their terrain as you zoom and pan, and keep a consistent scale as the game crosses a zoom level.
- **Picture options.** Render Resolution offers Original, 2×, 4× and Window. The GPU draws the landscape at the selected resolution while preserving the original sprite pixels. STE Detail adds stepped grass strokes and moving gusts. Retro Waves is a separate, optional water effect.
- **Widescreen controls.** The pointer can cross the wider landscape. Commands still use the original play area. Use the overview map to bring a distant target into reach.
- **Intro, demo and scrolling.** Space or Enter skips the title. Attract mode shows the game's original demo picture, and loading a village restores the enhanced landscape. With the CRT bezel and Original mouse controls, moving the pointer to the screen edge scrolls the map and the wheel changes zoom.
- Hold the wheel button and drag to turn, or use the arrow keys to scroll. Skip Manual Check answers the printed-map question when starting a land. Turn it off in Game options to answer the question yourself.

### Star Wars and The Empire Strikes Back

- **Star Wars.** Smooth Motion carries the green trench walls, red panels and crossbars past the cockpit as you fly. The Death Star surface dots now follow the ground through steering and wraparound and continue into both widescreen views. If you load a Star Wars save made during Game Over, the cockpit returns at widescreen width, but scenery outside the original screen is missing. Reaching Game Over during play preserves that scenery.
- **The Empire Strikes Back.** The Executor's outline stays together during the introduction. Hoth ground markers follow the bank of the camera into the distance. The walkers' legs follow their joints through each pose. After Hoth, the distant Star Destroyer, turning TIE fighters and rotating projectiles move between game frames. The Game Over lettering appears over the widescreen scene.
- **Custom Soundtrack.** In F12, open Game options → Audio → Custom Soundtrack. Put your own MP3 or MIDI files in Add-ons/Star Wars or Add-ons/The Empire Strikes Back. You can replace individual scenes and keep the game's sound for the rest. Loading a save selects the music for the restored scene. For example, use trench.mp3 for the Death Star trench or walkers.mid for the Hoth walker battle.
- Empire can play one song continuously through the probe droids and walkers on Hoth, or from the Star Destroyer into the asteroid field. You can also supply separate tracks for those encounters and for Game Over.

### SemaOS

- The system views use new illustrations for the Master System, Game Gear and Amiga. SemaBench also uses the Amiga illustration in its desktop menu.

Download the ZIP for your desktop or the signed arm64 APK for Android. Existing desktop installations can use the built-in updater. Supply your own game files and optional music.
