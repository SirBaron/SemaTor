# 1.1.618 — Virus Remastered, quick presets, smoother Mega Drive

### Virus

- **Remastered.** A new look for David Braben's 1988 classic, every part its own switch in F12 > Game options. Sky with a sun glare and stars; climb into black space with a glowing atmosphere, dive back for a fiery re-entry. Haze, a real cloud layer you can fly through, rolling farmland with the virus creeping across it tile by tile, and a faceted sea with a smooth coastline. Sun lighting, soft sun shadows (Shadow Distance: Near, Medium, Far, Max), engine glow under the craft, and glow on bright things. HD models in the classic faceted style: trees, houses, shelters, pylons and a leaping fish; your craft keeps its original shape. Explosions, sparks and exhaust become soft glowing points.
- **New HUD.** The world goes fullscreen with a new overlay in three styles: Corners, Strip or Cockpit, all with live score, lives, missiles, fuel, altitude and the radar.
- The game itself is untouched: logic, physics, fuel, damage, score and sound stay the same. Every remaster option is off until you choose it.

### Quick presets for every game

- **F12 > Game options > Presets** now offers the first-start choices at any time: **Original** (the game as it was made), **Recommended** (the safe picks), **Everything** (every option at its highest setting) and, where a game has them, **Remastered**. Your choice is saved per game, and any option can still be changed by hand.
- Cheats and control preferences such as Invert Y Axis and Keep Upright stay as you set them in every preset.
- TurboLoad is now on by default for every game and in every preset, Original included. Turn it off in Game options if you want real-time loading.

### Mega Drive

- **Smooth Motion** keeps an even pace at 120, 144 Hz and above, with no repeated pictures, and the in-between pictures land exactly on time. Sonic and other games that move every frame no longer hitch. The default method is now FidelityFX optical flow; a method you already chose stays.
- **Widescreen.** Extended gameplay is now part of Widescreen itself, with no separate row. Rocket Knight Adventures keeps enemies alive across the whole wide view. Sonic 3 no longer stops at the end of Angel Island 2 in 21:9, and its bosses wake at their original distance. Sonic 2's Chemical Plant and Aquatic Ruin backdrops fill the wide margins. Road Rash II no longer flicks to 4:3 on hills, and the far road edge no longer turns white. Castlevania: Bloodlines is improved in the wide view.
- **Region.** A new Region option (Auto, USA, Europe, Japan) per cartridge, with PAL 50 Hz timing for Europe. Save states remember the region. Another World (Europe) now runs as a PAL game.

### Atari ST games

- **Powermonger.** Widescreen no longer flips back to 4:3 while you zoom. Wheel zoom and held scrolling are faster. Edge scrolling works on all four edges, with a new Edge Scroll Zone option (Default, Large, Small). Every map object now matches the buildings' scale.
- **Stunt Car Racer.** With Smooth Motion on, SemaTor now draws the track itself in 3D from the game's own track data, so the roads stay solid and still at any refresh rate (no more tearing). The distant hills stay whole, Smooth Motion keeps the display's rate, the GPU is ready from the start, and quitting is clean.
- **Star Wars.** In widescreen the towers at the sides appear in their places and can be shot.
- **Mega lo Mania.** The pointer no longer flies across the screen with Command Centre on.
- **Batman The Movie** stays in widescreen during action. **Infestation** keeps Smooth Motion on while you fire. **Armour-Geddon** keeps its enhancements when your vehicle is destroyed. **Starglider 2** gains a Draw Distance option.

### Android

- Game Gear, Master System, Mega Drive and Amiga games run smoother on older phones such as the Snapdragon 865. They now ask the phone for full speed, show exactly one game frame per screen refresh on 60/120 Hz screens, and stay off the slow cores.
- Frame generation is always off by default, and switches itself off if a phone can't keep up.
- Touch controls react faster when pressed.
- Screen looks say when a phone can't show them.

### SemaOS

- SemaBench: enhanced Amiga games now show in the Enhanced list and get the ENHANCED badge.
- The game catalogue lists all 29 enhanced Mega Drive games.
