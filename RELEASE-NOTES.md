## 1.1.622 — Star Fox 2, soundtrack packs and Thor support

- Star Fox 2 gains optional stage lighting, model shadows, hull reflections, smoother ship geometry and a widescreen HUD. Both Star Fox games can render flight at the fitted window resolution.
- Mega Drive MD+ and SNES MSU-1 soundtrack packs are supported. Black frame insertion and scanlines that follow the original system resolution are available across systems.
- The F11 and F12 menus share controls across systems. Game Details lists verified disk and cartridge filenames and hashes, and identifies editions with Enhanced profiles.
- The AYN Thor gets lower-screen controls, a keyboard and a trackpad. Mega lo Mania adds an interactive island map, sector reports and optional map scanlines on Android.
- Thor devices running the SemaOS microSD build based on 1.1.621 use the signed Linux ARM64 update. In SemaOS, choose Download update, then Install and restart. Battery and clock indicators are also available on desktop and handheld PCs.
- Xorg Unbound and the Dungeon Master replacement artwork are now separate optional add-ons.
